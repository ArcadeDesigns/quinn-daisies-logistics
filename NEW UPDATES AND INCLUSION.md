# Quinn Daisies — Website Content & Information Architecture

## 1. Website Positioning

### Primary Positioning

**Quinn Daisies is a cross-border logistics and trade execution company that enables international commerce through physical supply-chain management, verified sourcing, regulatory coordination, and in-market execution.**

### Core Business Model

The website should communicate three connected layers:

```text
                         QUINN DAISIES
                              │
          ┌───────────────────┼───────────────────┐
          │                   │                   │
          ▼                   ▼                   ▼
   LOGISTICS &          INTERNATIONAL        TECHNOLOGY &
   SUPPLY CHAIN         TRADE & SOURCING       VISIBILITY
     EXECUTION
          │                   │                   │
          └───────────────────┼───────────────────┘
                              ▼
                    TRADE EXECUTION
                    & MARKET ACCESS
```

### Priority of Emphasis

**1. Logistics & Supply Chain — Primary**

Physical movement, freight coordination, warehousing, customs, drayage, cargo handling, and destination delivery.

**2. International Trade & Sourcing — Co-Primary**

Commodity sourcing, supplier verification, procurement support, trade facilitation, market entry, and supply development.

**3. Compliance & Risk Management — Enabling Layer**

Documentation, quality verification, regulatory coordination, supplier due diligence, and trade compliance.

**4. Technology & Visibility — Enabling Layer**

Digital workflows, shipment visibility, documentation, customer communication, and operational intelligence.

The company should therefore **not present technology as a separate business competing with logistics**. Technology supports the physical operation.

## The Most Important Positioning Decision

I would **not** describe Quinn Daisies primarily as an "international trade company that also provides logistics."

That undersells what you are building.

The stronger positioning is:

> **Quinn Daisies is a logistics-led trade execution company.**

**Logistics is the operating engine.**

**International trade and sourcing are the commercial engine.**

**Compliance is the risk-control layer.**

**Technology is the visibility and scalability layer.**

That gives the entire website a much clearer architecture:

**LOGISTICS → TRADE → COMPLIANCE → VISIBILITY → MARKET ACCESS**

And the central promise becomes:

> **We take the operational complexity out of international trade by managing the physical movement, sourcing, compliance, and in-market execution required to get goods from origin to destination.**

This is substantially more coherent than presenting Quinn Daisies as five different businesses under one website.
